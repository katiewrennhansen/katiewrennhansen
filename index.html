About

My mission is to bring ideas to life through fast, polished web applications that work beautifully on every device.

I’m a freelance front-end engineer with over seven years of experience, including time as a Staff Engineer leading front-end work for a growing product team. I also have hands-on experience with design and back end development, so I can help carry your project from first idea to launch. I’ll explain things in plain language, keep you in the loop, and build something you can feel confident about.

I gravitated towards front-end development after realizing its perfect union of problem solving and visual creativity. Nothing beats the satisfaction when a tricky problem clicks, or the moment you see an idea come to life on screen.

Away from the keyboard, you’ll find me running, baking, reading, or traveling to all corners of the world.

Have an idea? I’d love to hear about it.
