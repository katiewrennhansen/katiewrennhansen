Services

Front-End Development

I build fast, accessible web apps with React, TypeScript, and Next.js, so your product feels smooth for users and is easy to grow. Whether you need a full project built or an extra senior engineer to join your team, I bring the experience of leading front-end work at a growing company.

Design-to-Code Implementation

I turn your Figma designs into pixel-accurate, responsive interfaces, and build reusable components with Storybook so your brand stays consistent and new pages come together faster as you grow.

Redesigns & Performance Improvements

If your current site is slow, outdated, or hard to use on mobile, I’ll modernize it, improving load times, accessibility, and usability so visitors stay and take action.

Web Apps & Dashboards

From customer portals to data dashboards, I build interactive tools that make complex information clear and easy to use. I can also connect them to your APIs and handle light back-end work when needed.

About

My mission is to bring ideas to life through fast, polished web applications that work beautifully on every device.

I’m a freelance front-end engineer with over seven years of experience, including time as a Staff Engineer leading front-end work for a growing product team. I also have hands-on experience with design and back end development, so I can help carry your project from first idea to launch. I’ll explain things in plain language, keep you in the loop, and build something you can feel confident about.

I gravitated towards front-end development after realizing its perfect union of problem solving and visual creativity. Nothing beats the satisfaction when a tricky problem clicks, or the moment you see an idea come to life on screen.

Away from the keyboard, you’ll find me running, baking, reading, or traveling to all corners of the world.

Have an idea? I’d love to hear about it.
