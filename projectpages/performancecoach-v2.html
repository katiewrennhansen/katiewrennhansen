The Performance Coach

Overview

The Performance Coach is a sports and athletic training business that needed a clear online home. I built a responsive website that helps a personal trainer promote his services, training philosophy, and availability while making it easy for clients to contact him directly.

The challenge

Clients needed to see when he was available without waiting on a developer, and he needed to keep his information current without managing a complicated scheduling system. The site also needed to feel polished and easy to navigate on mobile and desktop.

The solution

I embedded a Google Calendar into the site so the trainer could manage his availability directly and keep the calendar up to date without relying on manual changes. This made the booking info easy to update and ensured clients saw the latest schedule. The site is responsive and designed to keep the most important information clear on smaller screens.

Adapting as needs changed

The payment page initially included a dropdown for services, but the client later decided he preferred to speak with people before purchase. I removed the checkout flow and shifted the focus to a direct contact path, keeping the site aligned with his business model and making the conversion path simpler.

Working with a designer

I built the site in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript based on designs from a web designer who had worked with the client. The layout is clean and lightweight, with a strong emphasis on readability and quick access to key information.
